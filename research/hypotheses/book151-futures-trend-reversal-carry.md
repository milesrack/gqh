## Source, mechanism and finite trials

The supplied Kakushadze and Serur (2018), *151 Trading Strategies*, is the primary source. Read §10.3 (printed p95/PDF p96), §10.4 (p96/p97), and §9.1 (p89/p90) directly from the extracted original. This source describes strategies; its statements are not accepted as performance evidence.

1. **Trend**: institutional flows and slow information incorporation may produce persistent price changes. Trading counterparties are hedgers and discretionary participants adjusting exposure gradually; persistence is a hypothesis, not established evidence. ES/NQ/ZN/CL/GC/6E; sign of cumulative causal point changes over 60, 120, 240 observed daily bars; trailing 63-bar point-change volatility; rebalance first eligible UTC weekday in each month. Three trials. Base neighbour is 120, selected only on training. This familiar risk premium must beat static exposures after costs and show incremental value over its own 120-day control before being described as differentiated alpha.
2. **Weekly reversal**: temporary inventory/relative demand shocks may reverse after liquidity providers absorb pressure. Same six roots. Sum causal daily point changes over 5, 10, 20 bars divided by trailing 63-bar point-change volatility; demean across roots; negative residual is signal. This is the book's relative reversal adapted to risk units, not raw percentage returns across unlike assets. Rebalance first eligible UTC weekday each week. Three trials. A slow secular shift or compensation for beta falsifies the proposed temporary-pressure interpretation. Do not add volume/open-interest filters.
3. **Curve carry**: producers and consumers may pay speculators to warehouse inventory/demand risk; relative scarcity is reflected in the curve. CL/GC only. At information time select nearest and next dated maturities with expiry at least 40 calendar days away, both positive prices and positive prior volume; reject if either is missing. Score is annualised slope 365*(Pfront-Pnext)/(abs(Pfront)*(expiry_next-expiry_front).days). Cross-sectional demeaning produces opposite CL/GC signals. Trade each root's eligible liquid contract, not a free price-gap profit. Weekly and monthly rebalance: two trials. Annualisation adapts the book's Pfront/Pnext rule to unequal maturity gaps. Two commodities cannot establish broad commodity carry; capital/risk behaviour of CL versus GC can dominate. No additional maturities, calendar spreads or tuning.

Exactly 8 primary candidate trials. No new variants after validation. A fixed equal-risk long portfolio of all six roots, equal-risk ES-only, equal-risk CL/GC long and fixed 120-day trend are controls, not selectable candidates. Training-only family selection chooses highest net annual Sharpe among finite menu; ties within 0.05 favour 120-day trend, 10-day reversal, monthly carry. Do not nominate a family unless training net Sharpe>0.25 and doubled-cost mean>0. The complete training menu and all failures remain visible. If no family qualifies, stop without opening validation.

GLBX.MDP3 `ohlcv-1d` is a UTC aggregate, not settlement or exchange-session data. A bar dated d is fully available only after d ends. Decisions for execution at d use completed data no later than d-2; d-1 is an extra latency buffer. Fill at d's first reported trade (open), plus one native tick slippage per side. Position P&L runs from actual contract open to next actual contract open. Use UTC weekdays as the observation/execution grid, with Sunday bars retained in raw evidence but not used as a separate return observation or rebalance. Features use weekday closes only. Weekend gaps accrue to held positions between Friday and Monday observed opens; no Sunday mark or synthetic fill is inserted. This keeps the fixed 252 annualisation basis consistent with the observation grid. No close-known-at-open fills, daily-bar stops, high/low fill choices or posthoc deletion of gap losses. The final validation positions close at the last available development open; no later price is requested.

## Actual contracts, rolls and data validity

Raw DBN originals are immutable; manifests retain requests, retrieval UTC times, provider version, SHA256 and row/date/instrument coverage. Parent OHLCV covers all outright chains; exclude spreads/combos using raw-symbol grammar and definitions. Continuous mappings are identification aids only: Databento continuous prices are unadjusted and cannot be differenced across different instruments. Instrument IDs may be reused: join by dated validity intervals.

At d choose the highest-volume eligible actual dated contract using volume known at d-2, with expiry at least 40 calendar days after d; choose only contracts whose definitions were available by the decision time. Roll only forward in maturity; incumbent remains until a later maturity has greater lagged volume or incumbent enters the 40-day exclusion window. Never revert to an earlier maturity. Calendar expiry fields are not assumed equivalent to first-notice dates: 40 days is a conservative screening buffer; independently verify notice/delivery rules before any deployment or acceptance. If 40 days is not safely before notice, that root fails this screen rather than reducing the buffer.

At roll open close the incumbent and enter replacement; preserve old-contract open and new-contract open, fees and slippage for both sides. Define historical increment at bar h using the contract selected causally at h-1 and that same dated contract's closes at h,h-1. Its availability is after h ends. Execution at d consumes increments only through d-2, so no close from d-1 enters a decision. Sum these within-contract differences into a causal additive price-change history. No full-sample adjustment, synthetic roll returns or future volume ranks. Missing held-contract liquidation/open prices invalidates the affected experiment; no silent flat return or interpolation. Holidays with no trades are non-events but positions remain held. Unexpected disappearing contracts, expired holdings, ambiguous identities or cross-root incomplete signal sets are recorded failures, with no substitutions.

## Portfolio, costs and executable risk

Initial equity USD 1,000,000; trade whole standard contracts, truncating target magnitude towards zero. Positions are sized by trailing 63-bar point-change covariance in dollar terms, using minimum 40 observations and no fit beyond information time. Signal weights are signal/individual dollar volatility, then scaled so trailing predicted portfolio annual volatility is 10%; shrink correlation matrix 50% towards diagonal as one fixed regularisation rule. No fitted optimiser. Maximum gross notional 100% equity; per-root notional 25%; combined ES/NQ 35%. Scale before rounding. Carry each root maximum 50%, gross 100%. Book familiar weights are adapted to capital constraints and documented as such.

Conservative reserved margin is 20% gross absolute notional, at most 50% equity; this is a stress assumption, not verified historical broker margin. Idle cash earns 0; report unlevered cash-zero benchmark and disclose treasury yield omission. Mark open P&L using contract multiplier; do not add roll gap. Fixed multipliers/native ticks subject to CME/dated-definition verification: ES 50/0.25,NQ 20/0.25,ZN 1000/1/64,CL 1000/0.01,GC 100/0.1,6E 125000/0.00005. ZN prices are quoted in points; 1/64 point is USD 15.625. Any mismatch blocks that root rather than guessing scaling.

Base scenario USD 2.50 commission/contract/side plus 1 tick/side; all entries, exits, size changes and both roll legs. Sensitivity doubles commission and ticks. Broker commissions and first-trade impact are scenario assumptions. Reject fills requiring more than 1% of lagged 20-bar average daily contract volume; report minimum participation-constrained capital bound. Daily realised equity loss>3% triggers liquidation at next available open and 5 eligible-bar pause, recording any additional gap loss; no invented intraday maximum-loss guarantee. Equity drawdown>20% from peak triggers the same fixed pause; never assume a stop filled at an unavailable price.

Compare paired daily excess returns versus specified controls. Use 10000 moving-block bootstrap replicates, block 20 observed portfolio bars, seed 151, for mean and paired incremental mean 95% intervals; report serial dependence and the 8-trial search as limitations. A good result requires positive doubled-cost validation mean, net Sharpe>=0.5, positive lower 95% mean interval, nonnegative immediate neighbours and no single-root contribution>70% of absolute total. These are screening gates, not proof. Claim incremental alpha only if paired excess lower 95% interval is also>0 against the relevant fixed control. Report failures immediately; no salvage variants or final holdout. If a gate passes, notify parent promptly with uncertainty, doubled costs and neighbours.
