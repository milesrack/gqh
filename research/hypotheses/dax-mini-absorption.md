# Mini passive liquidity absorption

Aggressive orders may reflect execution deadlines rather than superior information. Substantial one-sided volume consumed without appreciable price movement, while opposing touch depth is restored, can reveal resilient liquidity supply. Continued inventory transfer may leave aggressors exposed to a subsequent reversal. Public book observations identify absorption; they do not identify the supplier or its motive.

H0: Absorption adds no executable negative subsequent midpoint drift relative to equally strong aggressive flow or price-only reversal. H1: Strong aggressive flow against replenished, price-stable liquidity predicts reversal sufficient to pay liquid-contract marketable costs.
