# DAX ranked equity and futures pilot

Hypothesis: asymmetric demand across large German equities contains information about intraday index demand. Rank prior-close momentum, market-residual momentum or volatility-normalised momentum. Trade equal-weight top/bottom stock baskets; separately trade DAX futures using ranked-tail asymmetry and market breadth. Null: neither mechanism produces positive incremental net returns against matched market exposure.

Freeze six lookbacks, three quantiles, three ranking families, both signs, three rebalance frequencies and two cost scenarios. Equity baskets are long top-ranked and short bottom-ranked, reversed for contrarian jobs. Futures leadership is the sign of upper-tail plus lower-tail mean score minus twice the cross-sectional median. Futures breadth is the sign of the fraction of positive scores minus one-half. Signals use previous-session closes. No intraday stock return enters the day's signal.

Use the existing March–July 2025 futures pilot, ending before the locked 28 July final holdout. Stocks are a fixed 30-name German large-cap subset, not a point-in-time DAX membership history. Yahoo adjustments are retrieved retrospectively. These limitations prevent a historical constituent-alpha or competition-validation claim. Both chronological segments are development evidence because futures outcomes have already been inspected. Freeze the first 80% of common pilot sessions for selection and the remaining 20% for diagnostic reporting; do not rerank on that segment.

Equity sleeve: daily open-to-close returns, positions retained across decision sessions but liquidated overnight; charge both entry and exit each day and intraday borrow accrual. Rankings update every 1/3/5 common sessions. Futures sleeve: one contract, observed 09:10–17:20 Europe/Berlin quotes, crossing spread at both ends plus one point slippage and €2 per side. FDAX/FDXM/FDXS point values are €25/€5/€1. Contract mapping and quote provenance come from the frozen original pilot. No overnight futures position or invented roll return.

Save every trial, failures, date-level returns, data hashes and training sensitivity tables. No new paid request. Report risk, turnover, benchmarks and uncertainty before treating a screen as alpha. This pilot models stock borrow cost but does not establish borrow availability, exchange fees or margin funding.
