# Volume-conditioned futures reversal

Unexecuted finite exploratory adaptation of Kakushadze/Serur2018 §10.3.1 (printed p95/PDF p96). Parent must commit before outcomes. Three variants only; no additional purchases, markets or final data.

Mechanism: unusually intense trading can accompany temporary liquidity demand and inventory pressure, producing short-lived relative price displacement. Volume is not evidence that a trader is a hedger or forced seller. Conditional reversal must improve on the unfiltered reversal after costs; otherwise reject the mechanism and stop. Open interest is unavailable, so this omits the book's open-interest filter and is labelled an adaptation.

Universe ES/NQ/ZN/CL/GC/6E and the approved dated-contract development history,2015-01-01 to2024-05-27 exclusive. Training ends2022-01-19 exclusive; validation ends2024-05-27 exclusive; final remains locked. Reuse the futures preregistration's actual-contract selection, d−2 information buffer, within-contract point-change history, weekday observation grid, whole-contract sizing, risk/capital/participation constraints, fees, rolls, liquidation rules and dependency checks without amendment, including the pre-outcome historical tick correction addendum and its actual dated cost schedule. No return differencing across rolled instruments. No live trading.

At each first eligible weekday of the week, base score is EXACTLY the unfiltered5-bar engine calculation: first divide each root’s sum of the previous5 eligible daily point changes by its trailing63-bar point-change standard deviation, then demean those normalised values across the six roots and negate them. Never demean raw point changes across contracts with different units. Use the same fixed5-bar unfiltered reversal engine and require all six roots to have valid inputs. For volume at historical bar h, sum traded contracts across all outright dated instruments belonging to that root using definitions available no later than h's information timestamp. Exclude only confirmed spreads. An unidentified instrument or unknown historical root membership fails the decision and dataset coverage gate; never silently remove it. Do not select contracts by later volume, later expiry, today's chain membership or future definitions. Use every known outright, not just the selected rolling contract, so rolls between maturities do not create fictitious activity changes.

At decision time d use only volume bars through d−2. Let V be the sum over the latest5 eligible weekday bars and Vprev the preceding5. If Vprev is zero or either week is incomplete, fail that decision rather than impute volume. Activity ratio A=V/Vprev. Admit a root if A>=k, where the fixed candidate menu is k={1,1.5,2}. No top-half rank filter. Multiply the already demeaned base score by the admission mask; do not demean again. At least two roots must remain admitted with nonzero scores, otherwise hold cash. Use the existing portfolio-risk sizing engine on these masked scores; report resulting net exposure and root contributions.

Control: the exact unfiltered5-bar reversal, with the same six-root eligibility, warmup, risk constraints, cash yield, fills and costs; also report static equal-risk exposure and cash. No parameter or family substitution after validation. Freeze the eligible training candidate with highest net Sharpe; ties within0.05 favour k=1. Family eligibility: training Sharpe>0.25, doubled-cost mean>0, at least30 actual portfolio reallocations and positive return in at least3 calendar years. If none qualifies, stop without validation.

Validation: selected cutoff and the two declared neighbours, doubled commission/tick costs and one additional eligible-bar delay. Recompute each control using matching scenario costs and delay. Report portfolio net mean/CAGR/Sharpe/drawdown, turnover, roll and trade counts, costs, exposure, roots and yearly contributions. Use paired20-bar moving-block bootstrap10000draws, seed151; report95% and3-candidate-adjusted incremental mean intervals. Positive finding requires selected doubled-cost mean>0, Sharpe>=0.5, positive lower95% mean and paired-control incremental intervals, nonnegative both neighbours and no root>70% absolute total contribution. Raw positive returns or exposure reduction are insufficient. No final holdout access. Record every failure and reject without tuning.

Prerequisites: complete dated futures development data and independently audited execution engine; causal whole-root volume/definition membership must be reproducible. Missing membership history or ambiguous roll/volume records fails this experiment before outcomes. Additional variants require a separate prior specification, not salvage edits.
