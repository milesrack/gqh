# Persistent informed flow with confirmed price discovery

Large orders are split to control impact. Repeated aggressive trading across FDAX and FDXM, accompanied by price movement rather than complete absorption, may identify continuing demand. FDXS counterparties provide immediacy and inventory adjustment; subsequent flow must move prices enough to pay its spread. Public prints identify a state proxy, not participant identity.

H0: Conditioning on cross-contract flow persistence adds no positive executable validation return beyond own-contract momentum and a price-only trend baseline. H1: Confirmed same-direction flow across successive windows predicts continued price discovery over 30–300 seconds and produces net returns after observed spreads and fees.

Use the existing matched-maturity 97-session cache only. Training 10 March–20 June 2025; validation 23 June–25 July; never inspect 28 July onward. Select contracts with the existing causal roll rule. Session 09:05–17:25 Europe/Berlin. Quote snapshots require publisher 101, completed event flag 128, no quality flags 12, positive price/depth and non-crossed book. Quotes age at decision at most one second. Flow uses trade action T, side B/A only.

Finite menu: lookback 30 or 60 seconds; holding period 60, 180 or 300 seconds; imbalance threshold 0.4 or 0.6. Grid decisions every 10 seconds. Require FDAX and FDXM signed imbalance in both consecutive lookback windows to exceed threshold with the same sign, at least five classified trades per product/window, and FDAX and FDXM midpoint changes over the two windows each strictly in that direction. Require FDXS current spread at most three index points. Twelve candidates. Fixed direction is continuation. No reversal or validation-driven extra filter.

Controls: same lookback/horizon price-only common direction for FDAX/FDXM; own-FDXS price momentum with matching two-window positive changes and spread eligibility. Neither uses cross-flow. Compare selected flow candidate against both controls at its fixed timing parameters.

Execute one FDXS at first valid observed quote after decision plus 100 ms; buy ask/sell bid and opposite-side exit at holding time plus 100 ms. No overlapping positions or overnight trades; 20-point adverse-midpoint stop with same latency; daily realised loss cap EUR 100. Missing exit invalidates the run rather than deleting a trade. Fees EUR 1/side primary, EUR 0.5/2 scenarios. No optimistic passive fills.

Rank twelve candidates using training mean daily net EUR at EUR 1/side, requiring at least 100 training trades and positive training mean. Freeze the highest-ranked eligible candidate before any validation evaluation. If none qualifies, reject this family without validation tuning. Record all training variants. Validation checks fixed chosen candidate and neighbouring declared candidates, 250 ms latency, doubled observed spreads and fees, EUR 2/side, daily five-day circular block-bootstrap mean CI (2,000 draws, seed 20261003), day coverage and trade counts. Validation profitability is exploratory evidence, not final holdout proof. Reject a significant good claim if baseline outperforms, CI includes zero, doubled costs eliminate the effect, or neighbouring candidates reverse it.
