# Execution-contract cost efficiency

Persistent-flow training showed positive executable gross movement that Micro fees removed. An identical directional forecast can have different net value across contract sizes because fixed per-contract fees and quoted index-point spreads differ. This execution hypothesis follows the failed persistent-flow training study; it is not an independent information discovery. Large-print continuation has failed validation and is excluded.

Keep the original twelve persistent-flow candidates unchanged: two consecutive 30/60-second windows, same-direction FDAX/FDXM imbalance at 0.4/0.6, at least five trades per window, both products' positive directional midpoint response, Micro spread <=3 points, holds 60/180/300 seconds, 10-second decision grid. All quote/expiry/split restrictions remain. Training March 10–June 20; validation June 23–July 25; final holdout locked.

Execute one matched Mini or large contract, marketable first valid quote at decision+100ms and opposite-side exit after the fixed hold+100ms. EUR5/point Mini; EUR25/point large. Primary illustrative fees EUR1/side Mini and EUR2/side large. No overlap/overnight. Fixed stop20 index points gives EUR100/500 nominal stop exposure. Daily realised loss limits EUR500/2,500 and reporting capital EUR50,000/250,000 preserve the Micro experiment's index exposure relative to capital. No broker margin or live capital feasibility claim. Reject missing exits; retain delayed/adverse exits.

H0: Unchanged flow rules have non-positive net return on both larger contracts, or do not outperform price-only and own-contract momentum. H1: Lower fee and spread cost per index point permits positive executable net drift from the same information rule.

Twenty-four training variants (twelve fixed signals by two execution products). Rank mean daily net return on proportional reporting capital; require >=100 training trades and positive mean. Freeze the chosen contract and parameters before validation. If none qualifies, reject.
