# Reusable futures framework: pre-outcome operational policy

Status: draft for independent review and commit before any market strategy outcomes. This is a new reusable-framework batch. The original eight-screen strict-selection batch remains recorded as a data/execution failure; its specification and evidence are preserved.

The metadata-only scan found no eligible replacement on 42 NQ dates through 19 May 2016 and 11 ZN dates through 19 February 2019. The strict nearest-two/forward-only rule also strands otherwise safe liquid incumbents on nine annual GC dates and two 6E dates. These observations concern contract identity, reported liquidity and delivery risk; no candidate returns were inspected.

Keep actual dated identities, received metadata, complete 20 observed common-calendar bars, ADV at least 100 contracts, nearest-two entry candidates, forward-only replacement and incumbent retention on equal ADV. An incumbent that remains independently eligible may continue outside the current nearest-two pair. This admits continuation of an existing contract only; new entries remain restricted to the nearest two eligible maturities. A missing required dated execution or held-contract bar remains fatal.

Use a 14 calendar-day expiry buffer for cash-settled NQ. This is a conservative operational rule, not an exchange-mandated threshold: [CME Chapter359 §§35902.G/35903](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/CME/IV/350/359/359.pdf) defines termination on the settlement morning and cash settlement. ZN must remain strictly before both actual last trade/expiry and its conservative delivery-risk bound: maturity-month start minus seven calendar days. ES, CL, GC and 6E retain the original 40-day expiry buffer; the existing conservative physical-delivery assertions also remain. The ZN bound is a conservative inference, not actual dated notice metadata: the [CME Treasury delivery guide, eighth edition, pp7–11](https://www.cmegroup.com/content/dam/cmegroup/trading/interest-rates/files/us-treasury-futures-delivery-process.pdf) places first position/intention two business days before the delivery month’s first business day. Month-start minus seven calendar days precedes that risk for the quarterly development calendars. No liquidity threshold, risk target, forecast or parameter menu changes.

Preserve the common UTC weekday calendar with the three previously frozen Good Friday closures. Reserve the first feature observation available under the extra-information-bar clock, then require the same 240 completed feature bars under that slower clock for both base and stress runs. All comparisons use the same execution dates and liquidate at the last common open. Manifest evidence records requested and effective dates. Windows without eligible observations fail rather than report a completed experiment.

A realised exposure-cap breach forces flat at the next eligible decision using the already observed breach state. Re-entry may occur on the subsequent eligible decision, subject to the existing risk rules. This adds no five-bar pause; that pause remains restricted to the previously specified loss/drawdown trigger. Targets never use the current execution open.

The metadata-only proposed-policy scan covers all 2,437 common weekday dates, with zero eligible/forward-path or required-execution-bar failures in every root after initial history formation. Evidence: `proposed-policy-metadata-scan.json`; the old 40-day eligibility-gap diagnostic is retained separately inside that file and must not be mistaken for a proposed-policy failure.

Everything else remains fixed: whole standard contracts, native dated multipliers/ticks, explicit commission/slippage on every traded leg, lagged forecast prices and covariance, risk-matched controls, training-only selection, one frozen validation release, all attempts in the ledger and final prices from 27 May 2024 locked. This policy is not authorised for live trading.
