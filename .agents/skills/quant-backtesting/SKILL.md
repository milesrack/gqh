# Quant backtesting

State exactly when inputs are observable, decisions are made, orders execute, and returns accrue. Prohibit look-ahead: a signal derived after an interval cannot earn that interval's return. Keep target signals, executed positions, turnover, gross PnL, costs, and net PnL distinct. Model transaction costs and slippage as configurable assumptions and report their units. Use fills consistent with the available data and market mechanics; reject impossible prices or volume. Define cash, leverage, financing, corporate actions, and rebalancing only when relevant. Test alignment and accounting with hand-computable synthetic cases.
