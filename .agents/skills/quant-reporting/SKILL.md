# Quant reporting

State the hypothesis, baseline, data and sample, assumptions, signal and portfolio rules, execution timing, validation design, results, limitations, and reproduction steps. Use the metrics and conventions in `docs/experiment-contract.md`; identify train, validation, and untouched test results separately. Explain costs, slippage, uncertainty, and failed robustness checks. Keep claims proportional to evidence and disclose selection/search scope. Check official submission rules when released; do not invent them or present synthetic unit-test outputs as competition results.
