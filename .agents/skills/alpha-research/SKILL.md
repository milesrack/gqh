# Alpha research

Treat momentum, mean reversion, volatility, cross-sectional signals, spreads or relative value, and regime filters as research families, not recommended strategies. For any candidate, explain the mechanism, required data, timing, universe, capacity/cost risks, and a falsifying test. Start with one simple baseline and predefine a limited comparison set. Separate exploratory findings from confirmatory holdout evidence; do not label in-sample profitability as alpha.
